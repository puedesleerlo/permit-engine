An AI system you can trace and audit

Permit Engine decides street-race permits with AI help. Every decision shows the evidence it read, the risk it calculated and the rule that fired. When the rules will not decide alone, an officer does.

    (1 − 0.05) × 0.96 = 0.912 of this case flows to one leaf: no confirmed escort, a good plan. The 0.05 is the escort answer after the contradicted letter, and the 0.96 comes from a decision model like Jev.

    0.120 × 0.02 + 0.598 × 0.10 + 0.282 × 0.30 = 0.147

    Rare, possible and likely stand for 0.02, 0.10 and 0.30, so this sum is the likelihood of a traffic conflict.

One sealed test, run once

The rule for passing was set before the test, and the test used cases the system had never seen. The system passed, and approvals with no officer rose from 47% to 57%.

It learns from every review

Each officer review moves the numbers inside the trees, only where that case passed through. The written rules never change by themselves.

  • Every officer review teaches the trees, whether it agrees or disagrees.
  • Written rules stay with people, and every change is logged.

Where this could apply next

Every decision here answers four questions: what supports it, what could go wrong, what the rules say, and whether it needs a person. So far it is built and tested for street races. Have a decision like these? Let’s map it to yours.

  • Banking

    A bank reviewing a loan

    What supports it?
    Income papers
    What could go wrong?
    Fraud, default
    What do the rules say?
    Lending rules
    Does it need a person?
    A credit analyst

  • Government

    An office granting a benefit

    What supports it?
    Claim papers
    What could go wrong?
    A wrong payment
    What do the rules say?
    The law
    Does it need a person?
    A caseworker

  • AI agents

    An agent about to move money or delete data

    What supports it?
    The request, its approvals
    What could go wrong?
    Money lost, data gone
    What do the rules say?
    Written limits
    Does it need a person?
    Its owner
